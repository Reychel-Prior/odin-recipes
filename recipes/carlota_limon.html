Carlota de limon

Description

A deliciously refreshing lime icebox cake that requires just four ingredients!

Ingredients

  • 2 cans evaporated milk
  • 2 cans sweetened condensed milk
  • 3/4 cup lime juice, or more to taste
  • 2/3 package Mexican galletas Maria

Steps

  1. Combine evaporated milk, condensed milk, and lime juice in a bowl; whisk until mixture is thick and smooth.
  2. Line a glass bowl or trifle dish with galletas Maria and cover them with a generous layer of the milk mixture. Add another layer of biscuits and milk. Continue the layering, ending with a layer of the milk mixture.
  3. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, but better if overnight. Remove from the fridge just before serving.
  4. Enjoy!
